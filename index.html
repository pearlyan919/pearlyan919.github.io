LLM Jailbreak & Security Evaluation

Independent Research · 2026–Present

Reproduced GCG jailbreak experiments and extended them with cross-model transfer and token-level truncation analysis. In a controlled 10-behavior comparison, GCG succeeded on 10/10 targets for Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct versus 1/10 for Llama-2-7B-Chat.

Transfer experiments further showed 100% transfer from Gemma-2 to the tested Llama models, while transfer in the reverse direction was only about 30%, revealing strong asymmetry across model families.
